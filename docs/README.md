## Overview

PillCrew is a free, browser-based platform that provides a **crew of ten AI
trading agents** for Solana memecoins. There is no account, wallet, or payment
required to analyze a token.

Every screen is backed by **live market data** (prices, market caps, volumes
and multi-timeframe moves refresh continuously.

**Live at [pillcrew.fun](https://pillcrew.fun)**

## Getting started

1. Open [pillcrew.fun](https://pillcrew.fun).
2. Pick an agent, or go directly to **Trending** / **Watchlist**.
3. Paste a token address or a `pump.fun/coin/...` link.
4. Request a full analysis, rug check, "buy or pass?", price target, safety
   audit or whale review. The agent answers in its own style.

## Live markets

- **Trending on Solana** - a live, sortable table of what is moving right now.
- **Crew Verdict** - the entire crew scans the trending coins and picks one
  winner with a full plan. Filters include market cap, coin age and minimum
  24-hour volume.
- **Charts** - open any coin's chart; on desktop you can enable a split view
  with a **swap widget next to the chart**. Clicking outside the chart
  minimizes it to a small floating widget instead of closing it.

## Alerts & watchlist

- Add coins to your **Watchlist** and configure ±% alerts over 1m, 5m, 15m,
  30m, 1h, 6h and 24h timeframes.
- Alerts are evaluated every minute; popups slide in from the bottom with
  **live-updating prices** and a dismiss button. Browser notifications are
  optional.
- The watchlist and alerts follow you across every page.

## Callouts

- **pump.fun** - brand-new launches streamed in near real time.
- **Trending** - DexScreener trending coins with live prices.
- Switch between the two sources with a single tap.

## FAQ

**Is an account required?**
**No.** The platform works without signing up.

**Is a wallet required?**
Only to execute swaps; all analysis is wallet-free.

**Is this financial advice?**
No. The platform is for education and entertainment only. Always conduct your
own research.

**How fresh is the data?**
Prices and market data refresh continuously across the application.
