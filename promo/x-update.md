## NEW — paste this first

**PillCrew 1.2.1 is out, and it is one program now.**

Pilly Desktop and PillCrew Sniper were the same idea with two installers and two settings files. They are now a single
app: the sniper, the bump desk, the AI council on your own keys, and Pilly walking on your taskbar — in one window, on
one wallet.

**What that means if you already run the sniper:** your profile, your settings and your wallet are copied across on the
first boot. Nothing to set up again, nothing deleted.

### What is inside

**The sniper.** Every new Pump.fun and PumpSwap coin is scored 0–100, four times a second, and exits are measured forty
times a second. Every refusal prints the sentence that says why — a quiet book is a decision, not a mystery.

**Practice Mode first.** 5 SOL of fake money, a separate ledger, one switch to real money when you have seen it work.

**The bump desk.** Volume for one token from wallets the app creates itself, through a single desk address. It tells you
what the whole run costs before a lamport moves — and it is honest about what it is: real buys and real sells, about 3.5 %
of each round trip going to the curve and the trade door.

**The council.** Your own DeepSeek or OpenRouter key, every usable endpoint asked in parallel, one VETO refusing the coin,
and a *Find free models* button that asks the provider what it actually offers today instead of trusting a name in the
code. Your keys stay on your disk and never enter a settings code.

**Pilly.** The whole mascot panel came across: his name, his colours, his size, how fast he walks, where he walks, how
often he stops, his bubbles and their styles, his voice, reduce motion, and six switches for what he may tell you — the
hot radar, fresh launches, the morning brief, his pick of the day, whale alerts, his mood. His **scorecard** keeps his
wins and his losses.

### Fixed in this update

- The chat printed the same sentence twice when only one model answered. It does not any more.
- Saving the look of the chat did nothing at all — the save threw before it wrote. It stores it now.
- Pilly's hop was drawn outside his own window, and his walk parked him in the corner of the screen. Both fixed: he stays
  in frame and turns around short of the edge.
- The scorecard's four numbers stacked in one column, and two chat colours used a violet that belongs to no other screen.
  Both fixed — the pictures in the repository are the proof.

### Install

Windows · macOS · Linux, no account, no subscription, open source.
