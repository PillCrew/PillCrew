Connection (RPC)

A private node (for example from Helius) makes the live feed and your transactions faster and unlocks the on-chain feed. It is tried first. Free public nodes are used as backup.

A Geyser-class stream delivers each transaction with its logs, which is the same program data this app decodes — only closer to the validator, so a launch is seen sooner. Leave it empty and the desk uses the public program-log feed exactly as before; if the endpoint stops answering, the desk falls back on its own.

Beginner Guide

Everything you need to know about Pump.fun in two minutes.

What is Pump.fun?

Pump.fun is a site where anyone can launch a meme coin on Solana in seconds. Thousands launch every day. Most go to zero, a few go up very fast.

What does this bot do?

It watches every new coin on Pump.fun and every trade it can see. It checks how many real people are buying, how fast the price moves, whether one wallet is faking volume and whether the creator dumped. If a coin scores high, it buys a small amount and sells automatically for profit or to cut losses.

Start safely

  1. Stay in Practice Mode and watch a few trades.
  2. Create your wallet in the Wallet tab and back up your key.
  3. Deposit a small amount of SOL.
  4. Switch to Real Money with the Balanced style.

Key words

SOL
The coin used to pay for everything on Solana.
Market cap
The total value of a coin. Low means early and risky.
Take profit
Sell automatically after a gain.
Stop loss
Sell automatically after a loss, so it cannot get worse.
Slippage
The most price movement you accept while buying or selling. Smart fees keeps it as tight as the coin allows.
Priority fee
A small tip that makes your transaction faster. Smart fees follows the live network price so you never overpay.
Private key
The secret that controls your wallet. Never share it.

Be honest with yourself about risk

Most meme coins lose value. Trading fees, network costs and fast price swings mean the bot can lose money even when it works as designed. Past results do not predict the future. Only use money you can afford to lose.

How the chat looks and answers

Which endpoint works with which AI?

Any OpenAI-compatible chat-completions API works: paste the address into Endpoint, the key into Key, and the model name into Model. The council tries your endpoints in order and asks every usable one at once.

OpenRouter https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/chat/completions — free models end with :free (a model name is required). Groq https://api.groq.com/openai/v1/chat/completions. Cerebras https://api.cerebras.ai/v1/chat/completions. NVIDIA NIM https://integrate.api.nvidia.com/v1/chat/completions. DeepSeek https://api.deepseek.com/chat/completions. Together https://api.together.xyz/v1/chat/completions. Mistral https://api.mistral.ai/v1/chat/completions.

A local server works too: Ollama or LM Studio on http://localhost:11434/v1. Keys are stored on this computer only and are sent straight to the provider you configure — never to us, because there is no us in the path.
